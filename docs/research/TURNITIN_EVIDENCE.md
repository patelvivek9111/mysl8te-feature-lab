## Academic Research Papers

### Turnitin Similarity Detection Research

| Citation | URL/Source | Evidence Type | Key Findings |
|----------|------------|---------------|--------------|
| Weber-Wulff et al. (2009). "An Empirical Research Study of the Efficacy of Two Plagiarism-Detection Applications" | https://doi.org/10.1080/19322900903051011 | DOCUMENTED | 82.4% detection rate, 16.8% false positive rate, close review essential |
| Walchuk, K. (2016). "An Examination of the Efficacy of the Plagiarism Detection Software Program Turnitin" | University of Ontario Institute of Technology thesis | DOCUMENTED | Effective for verbatim, inconsistent for paraphrasing, gap in subscription content, coincidental matches common |
| Bertram Gallant et al. (2019). "Plagiarism or Not? Investigation of Turnitin-Detected Similarity Hits" | https://bozinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Plagiarism-or-Not-Investigationof-Turnitin%C2%AE-Detected-Similarity-Hitsin-Biology-Laboratory-Reports.pdf | DOCUMENTED | Average 29 hits but only 2 real problems, 53% had source issues, most not plagiarism |
| Stapleton, P. (2012). "Gauging the Effectiveness of Anti-Plagiarism Software" | Journal of English for Academic Purposes, v11 n2 | DOCUMENTED | Deterrent effect demonstrated, raw percentages don't always reflect intentional plagiarism |
| Scoping Review (2021). "Text-Matching Software Used for Student Academic Integrity" | https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/4834860 | DOCUMENTED | 129 studies reviewed, no conclusive evidence text-matching alone reduces plagiarism |
